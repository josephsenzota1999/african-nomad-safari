Ngorongoro Conservation Area

A vast volcanic landscape where crater walls shelter one of Africa's most remarkable concentrations of wildlife.

Home > Destinations > Ngorongoro

A natural sanctuary in the crater highlands

The Ngorongoro Conservation Area is one of Tanzania's most extraordinary places. At its heart, the Ngorongoro Crater is the world's largest intact volcanic caldera, with steep walls rising around a fertile floor of grassland, forest, lakes, and marshes.

Descending into the crater feels like entering a complete wildlife world. Lions, elephants, hippos, buffalo, flamingos, and the rare black rhino can all be found in a single day, while the surrounding highlands add Maasai culture, ancient archaeological sites, and dramatic walking routes.

Highlights of Ngorongoro

  • Game drives across the crater floor
  • Strong chances of seeing black rhino
  • Lion prides, elephants, buffalo, and hippos
  • Flamingos and birdlife around Lake Magadi
  • Maasai communities and highland culture
  • Olduvai Gorge and early human history

Best time to visit

Ngorongoro is rewarding throughout the year. June to October usually brings dry weather and easy wildlife viewing, while November to May turns the highlands green and brings migratory birds. Early morning visits are best for soft light, cooler temperatures, and a quieter start on the crater floor.
