Northern Parks

Serengeti National Park

Serengeti National Park is Africa’s most famous park, hosting the largest concentration of migratory game on the continent and the spectacular Great Wildebeest Migration. With over 14,750 square kilometers of endless plains, it offers world-class viewing of predators (lions, leopards, cheetahs) and giant herds tracking rain patterns year-round.

Northern Parks

Ngorongoro Conservation Area

The Ngorongoro Crater is a UNESCO World Heritage site and the world’s largest intact volcanic caldera. Often called the 'Eighth Wonder of the World', its steep 600-meter walls enclose a fertile floor packed with over 25,000 large mammals, including prides of lions, black rhinos, hippos, and giant elephant bulls feeding in marshes.

Highland Peaks

At high altitude, East Africa offers some of the world’s most memorable summit adventures. Trekking through dramatic landscapes from forest to alpine zones delivers a bucket-list experience that combines challenge, scenery, and unforgettable views.

Northern Parks

Tarangire National Park

Tarangire National Park is famous for its iconic, ancient baobab trees and having the largest concentration of wild elephants in East Africa. During the dry season (June to October), the Tarangire River serves as the only water source for miles, drawing herds of up to 300 elephants, alongside migratory zebras, wildebeests, and stalking lions.

Other Places

Masai Mara National Reserve

Located just across the border in southwestern Kenya, the Masai Mara borders the Serengeti to form a continuous wildlife ecosystem. Renowned for its exceptional population of big cats and the dramatic Mara River crossing during the Great Migration, it offers a legendary safari experience on classic acacia-dotted hills.

Southern Parks

Mikumi National Park

Tanzania’s fourth-largest park, Mikumi offers open horizons and rich wildlife that draw frequent comparisons to the Serengeti. The Mkata floodplains are a highlight, harboring herds of elands (Africa's largest antelope), buffalos, zebra, elephants, wild dogs, and tree-climbing lions looking down from baobab branches.

View Southern Safaris
Eastern Parks

Saadani National Park

Saadani is a unique sanctuary—the only national park in East Africa that borders the warm waters of the Indian Ocean. It allows travelers to track big game like elephants, lions, and giraffes in the morning, and spend the afternoon relaxing on pristine white beaches under coconut palms listening to breaking waves.
