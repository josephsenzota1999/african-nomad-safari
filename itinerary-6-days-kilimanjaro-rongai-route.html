6 Days Kilimanjaro Rongai Route

Duration: 6 Days / 5 NightsType: Kilimanjaro TrekkingRoute: Rongai Route & Uhuru Peak

This 6-day Kilimanjaro Rongai Route climb is designed for trekkers seeking a quieter, more scenic ascent with strong summit support. Starting on the northern side, the route offers peaceful trekking through rainforest, moorland and alpine desert.

Your guide team handles logistics, meals, and safety support so you can focus on the climb and reach Uhuru Peak with confidence.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Rongai Gate to Simba Camp
The trek begins at Rongai Gate after a scenic drive through the Kenyan border region. Hike gently through the rainforest and arrive at Simba Camp in the afternoon.

Elevation: 2000 m to 2625 m · Time: 4-5 hours · Distance: 8 km · Accommodation: Simba Camp

Day 2: Simba Camp to Second Cave
Cross the moorland zone and enjoy stunning views of Kibo Massif. Reach Second Cave by late afternoon and relax at camp.

Elevation: 2625 m to 3450 m · Time: 5-6 hours · Distance: 9 km · Accommodation: Second Cave

Day 3: Second Cave to Kikelewa Camp
This shorter day takes you deeper into the alpine desert, passing giant lobelias and groundsels on the way to Kikelewa Camp.

Elevation: 3450 m to 3600 m · Time: 5-6 hours · Distance: 7 km · Accommodation: Kikelewa Camp

Day 4: Kikelewa Camp to Mawenzi Tarn
Ascend toward the jagged peaks of Mawenzi, arriving at scenic Mawenzi Tarn Camp by afternoon. This is one of the most dramatic and memorable sections of the Rongai Route.

Elevation: 3600 m to 4330 m · Time: 4-5 hours · Distance: 6 km · Accommodation: Mawenzi Tarn Camp

Day 5: Mawenzi Tarn to Kibo Hut
Trek across the high-altitude Saddle between Mawenzi and Kibo. The terrain becomes more barren, and you’ll reach Kibo Hut in the afternoon to rest before the summit push.

Elevation: 4330 m to 4700 m · Time: 5-6 hours · Distance: 9 km · Accommodation: Kibo Hut

Day 6: Kibo Hut to Uhuru Peak, descend to Horombo Hut
Begin the summit attempt at midnight and climb to Uhuru Peak for sunrise. After celebrating at the summit, descend to Horombo Hut for your final mountain night.

Elevation: 4700 m to 5895 m summit, descend to 3720 m · Time: 12-14 hours · Distance: 22 km · Accommodation: Horombo Hut
