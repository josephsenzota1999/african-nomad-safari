8 Days Lemosho Route

Duration: 8 Days / 7 NightsType: Kilimanjaro TrekkingDestinations: Lemosho Route & Uhuru Peak

The 8-Day Lemosho Route is a perfect Kilimanjaro summit adventure for travelers seeking a less crowded route with strong acclimatization. Beginning on Kilimanjaro’s lush western slopes, this itinerary crosses rainforest, the Shira Plateau, and alpine desert before reaching Uhuru Peak.

This package gives you a balanced climb with extra time to adjust to altitude, improving summit success while letting you enjoy the mountain’s dramatic landscapes and quieter campsites.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Moshi
Arrive in Moshi, meet your guides, and receive a full trek briefing. Stay overnight in a comfortable hotel and prepare for the mountain journey.

Meals: None · Accommodation: Moshi hotel

Day 2: Londorossi Gate to Mti Mkubwa Camp
Transfer to Londorossi Gate, register with park authorities, and trek through rainforest to Mti Mkubwa Camp. This first day introduces you to Kilimanjaro’s lush western slopes.

Distance: 7 km · Time: 3-4 hours · Habitat: Rainforest · Accommodation: Mti Mkubwa Camp

Day 3: Mti Mkubwa Camp to Shira 1 Camp
Leave the forest and climb onto the Shira Plateau. The route becomes drier and opens into expansive moorland before reaching Shira 1 Camp.

Distance: 8 km · Time: 5-6 hours · Habitat: Moorland · Accommodation: Shira 1 Camp

Day 4: Shira 1 Camp to Shira 2 Camp
A shorter trekking day allows you more time to acclimatize on the plateau. Enjoy wide views of Kibo Peak and the surrounding highlands.

Distance: 6 km · Time: 3-4 hours · Habitat: Moorland · Accommodation: Shira 2 Camp

Day 5: Shira 2 Camp to Barranco Camp via Lava Tower
Climb to Lava Tower for acclimatization, then descend to Barranco Camp in the dramatic Barranco Valley—a key high-altitude training day.

Distance: 10 km · Time: 7-8 hours · Habitat: Semi-desert · Accommodation: Barranco Camp

Day 6: Barranco Camp to Karanga Camp
Climb the Barranco Wall and cross Karanga Valley, then continue to Karanga Camp. This route offers excellent views of the south glacier and Kibo’s eastern slopes.

Distance: 5 km · Time: 4-5 hours · Habitat: Alpine desert · Accommodation: Karanga Camp

Day 7: Karanga Camp to Barafu Camp
A shorter day brings you to Barafu Camp, the final high camp before the summit push. Rest and prepare for the early morning ascent.

Distance: 4 km · Time: 3-4 hours · Habitat: Alpine desert · Accommodation: Barafu Camp

Day 8: Barafu Camp to Uhuru Peak & Mweka Camp
Start before midnight and climb to Uhuru Peak for sunrise. After celebrating at the highest point in Africa, descend to Mweka Camp for your final mountain night.

Distance: 5.7 km ascent, 12 km descent · Time: 12-14 hours · Habitat: Arctic / Alpine desert · Accommodation: Mweka Camp
