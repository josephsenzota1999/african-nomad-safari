African Nomad Safari was founded in 2017 by Michael Leopaord and Joseph Genesis. Offering specialized East African private tours, our English-speaking expert safari and travel guides provide unique travel experiences for memories that last a lifetime.

What we do

We plan and run private safaris across Tanzania and Kenya, with beach extensions in Zanzibar. Every itinerary is built from scratch, and every lodge we recommend has been visited by a member of our team. Around 100 properties across the region, from tented camps in the Serengeti to small hotels on the coast.

Our Story

African Nomad Safari was founded in 2017 by Michael Leopaord and Joseph Genesis, after a first private safari in the Serengeti showed them how much better the experience could be when it is built around one family at a time. The company has grown to a team of 30 across Arusha, Nairobi and the Netherlands, and has hosted guests from more than 40 countries.

Sustainability

We hold Travelife Partner status, an independently audited sustainability certification covering fair working conditions, community benefit and environmental impact. It applies to how we run the company, not just how we market it.

Our Offices

Our headquarters are in Tanzania, Arusha, Clock Tower Street. Our East Africa operations are based in Arusha, Tanzania, with support across the region. This local presence helps us provide responsive planning, on-the-ground coordination, and reliable support for every safari experience.

Guaranteed Security

Our payments are bonded under the Dutch SGR scheme, and someone from the team is reachable 24 hours a day while you are travelling. If you would like to discuss booking terms, get in touch.

How we work

Booking directly with the operator means fewer handovers and faster answers. Here is what that looks like in practice.

  1. A local operator, not a reseller
    Our own offices, guides and fleet of custom 4x4s. Nothing on your itinerary is subcontracted.
  2. Private, start to finish
    Your own guide and vehicle, at your own pace, with the freedom to change the day as you go.
  3. Fully accredited and bonded
    Payments are secured under the Dutch SGR scheme, with 24/7 support while you travel.

Recognition

African Nomad Safari has been honored with the prestigious TripAdvisor Travellers' Choice Award for 2026, placing us among the top 10% attractions worldwide. You can also view our thousands of independent reviews on Google and SafariBookings.com.
