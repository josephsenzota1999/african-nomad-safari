African Nomad Safari was founded in 2017 by Michael Leopaord and Joseph Genesis. Offering specialized East African private tours, our English-speaking expert safari and travel guides provide unique travel experiences for memories that last a lifetime.

Our Mission

Our mission has been the same from day one: to provide travelers with the best experience of East Africa possible. This extends to every aspect of the safari travel experience. From bespoke travel itineraries that tick all the boxes, to the handpicked lodges and luxury tented camps included on African Nomad trips. In fact, we partner with over 100 hotels, lodges, and luxury tented camps across Tanzania and Kenya, all personally assessed by our on-site team of travel professionals.

Our Story

Born from a passion for immersive, safe travel in East Africa, it took just one unforgettable private tour of the Serengeti to fall in love with the magic of Africa. Identifying a gap in the market for high-quality, fully customizable private tour operations, Michael Leopaord and Joseph Genesis founded African Nomad Safari to fill that gap. The next few years saw African Nomad Safari attract thousands of clients around the world to Africa's lush lands. The company now boasts a dedicated, full-time staff of 30 in Europe and East Africa, split between Arusha, Nairobi, and our headquarters in the Netherlands.

We're Proud to Have Achieved Travelife Partner Status

African Nomad has been awarded the Travelife Partner level: a globally recognized certification for sustainable travel practices. While not yet fully certified, this milestone reflects our ongoing commitment to protecting the environment, supporting local communities, and ensuring ethical travel experiences. From fair working conditions to minimizing our ecological footprint, every journey with us is designed with responsibility and impact in mind.

Our Offices

Our headquarters are in Tanzania, Arusha, Clock Tower Street. Our East Africa operations are based in Arusha, Tanzania, with support across the region. This local presence helps us provide responsive planning, on-the-ground coordination, and reliable support for every safari experience.

Guaranteed Security

Available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, when you book with African Nomad, you can book with total confidence. We are fully accredited and bonded under the Dutch SGR (ATOL equivalent in the UK), which secures all online payments. If you have any questions or would like to discuss booking options, get in touch with our team.

Why Travel with African Nomad Safari?

When it comes to bucket list travel, it's always better to deal with your operator directly. And when considering a completely bespoke safari vacation, dealing with trusted local experts is essential. At African Nomad, we pride ourselves on our dedication to customer service. Why? Because our mission is to make every visitor's experience in Africa as unforgettable as it can be.

  1. We are a local tour operator
    With our own offices, highly experienced team, and a fleet of robust, custom-built 4x4 safari vehicles, we are never dependent on third parties. We arrange and oversee your entire holiday from A to Z, making sure it's a smooth journey.
  2. We offer private safaris
    Our customized private safaris allow you to explore the best of Africa at your own pace. With your personal safari guide and private 4x4 vehicle, your trip is flexible and tailored to your interests.
  3. Our safari guides are world-class
    We pride ourselves on our professional guides. We put every member of our team through a rigorous selection process where we test local flora/fauna knowledge, tracking expertise, and customer care. Only the best of the best make it on to our team.
  4. We promise expert advice
    Our travel designers are experts in everything East Africa: luxury safaris, highland adventures, or coastal escape holidays. From planning the perfect highlights to advising on visas and packing lists.
  5. We always deliver impeccable value
    We offer high-quality private safaris at affordable prices. By operating directly and cutting out intermediate agents and commissions, we manage to keep our pricing highly competitive while passing the savings directly on to you.

The adventure starts with your ideas – we'll do the rest

Our website lets you browse popular trips and customizable options. Every itinerary is fully flexible: you decide how your journey looks, even down to what time your game drives start each day. Want to sleep in or spend all day out with a picnic lunch? No problem. Your vacation begins with what you want to experience, and we make it happen!

Looking for Travel Insurance?

Chapka offers comprehensive coverage tailored for safari travelers, groups, and families, ensuring you only pay for what you need during your African adventure. Enjoy 24/7 medical assistance for total peace of mind.
