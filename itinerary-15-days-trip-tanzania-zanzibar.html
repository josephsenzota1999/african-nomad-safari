15 Days Tanzania Safari & Zanzibar

Duration: 15 Days / 14 Nights Type: Private Safari & Beach Journey Destinations: Arusha, Tarangire, Serengeti, Ngorongoro & Zanzibar

This 15-day private journey combines Tanzania’s iconic wildlife circuit with a long beach finale on Zanzibar. It is ideal for travelers who want extra time in the bush, a slower pace, and a relaxing coastal ending after game drives and crater experiences.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Arrive in Arusha
Welcome to Tanzania and settle into your Arusha base with a smooth airport transfer and a relaxed evening.
Day 2: A relaxing day in Arusha
Enjoy a calm buffer day to recover from travel, explore the town, or simply relax at your lodge before the safari starts.
Day 3: Arusha National Park
Start your wildlife adventure with a day in Arusha National Park, known for its scenic landscapes, giraffes, and striking colobus monkeys.
Day 4: Leisure day or optional activities
Choose a slower day at the lodge or optional activities around Arusha such as hikes, local culture, or scenic outings.
Day 5: Tarangire National Park
Travel to Tarangire for classic elephant sightings, giant baobabs, and the riverine landscapes that make the park so special.
Day 6 - 8: Serengeti National Park
Spend several days exploring the Serengeti’s open plains, following migrating wildlife, and looking for predators against the dramatic backdrop of the grasslands.
Day 9: Ngorongoro Conservation Area
Visit the famous Ngorongoro Crater for one of the most extraordinary wildlife experiences in East Africa.
Day 10: Flight to Zanzibar
Fly to Zanzibar and swap the bush for palm-fringed beaches and a completely different pace.
Day 11 - 13: Zanzibar relaxation and exploration
Enjoy laid-back beach time, optional snorkeling, dhow cruises, or cultural visits to Stone Town and spice plantations.
Day 14 - 15: Departure from Zanzibar
Use the final days to enjoy the island one last time before your onward flight home.
