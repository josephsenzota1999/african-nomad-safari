Tarangire National Park

Ancient baobabs, wide savannah horizons, and some of East Africa's largest elephant gatherings.

Home > Destinations > Tarangire

Elephant country beneath the baobabs

Tarangire National Park is one of northern Tanzania's most distinctive safari landscapes. Its ancient baobab trees, rolling grasslands, seasonal swamps, and the Tarangire River create a striking setting for wildlife encounters away from the busier migration routes.

In the dry season, animals gather around the river and permanent wetlands. Large elephant herds are the signature sight, but game drives can also reveal lions, leopards, cheetahs, buffalo, giraffes, zebra, wildebeest, and more than 500 bird species.

Tarangire safari highlights

  • Large elephant herds along the Tarangire River
  • Ancient baobab trees and open savannah views
  • Tree-climbing lions and elusive leopards
  • Seasonal wetlands filled with birdlife
  • Quiet walking and cultural experiences nearby
  • Easy pairing with Ngorongoro and Serengeti

Best time to visit

June to October is the classic dry-season window, when wildlife concentrates around water and the park is especially rewarding for elephant viewing. November to May brings fresh green landscapes, young animals, and excellent birdwatching. Tarangire is also a strong choice for a quieter first or final stop on a northern circuit.
