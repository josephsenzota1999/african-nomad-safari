5 Days Marangu Route Kilimanjaro Climb

Duration: 5 Days / 4 NightsType: Trekking AdventureDestinations: Marangu Route & Uhuru Peak

This 5-day Marangu Route Kilimanjaro climb is a fast, comfortable option that uses hut accommodation and a direct summit push via Kibo Hut. You will trek from Marangu Gate through rainforest, moorland and alpine desert before reaching Uhuru Peak and descending back to the gate.

Highlights include the rainforest start, panoramic views of Mawenzi and Kibo, hut-style lodging, and a sunrise summit on Africa's highest peak. The itinerary is ideal for climbers on a tighter schedule who still want a full Kilimanjaro summit experience.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Marangu Gate to Mandara Hut
Register at Marangu Gate and begin your climb through lush rainforest. The hike ends at Mandara Hut for your first hut night on the mountain.
Day 2: Mandara Hut to Horombo Hut
Ascend into the Moorland Zone with sweeping views of Mawenzi and Kibo. You reach Horombo Hut in the afternoon for rest and acclimatization.
Day 3: Horombo Hut to Kibo Hut
Continue through alpine desert terrain toward Kibo Hut, the final resting point before the summit push. Enjoy a quiet evening and prepare for the midnight ascent.
Day 4: Kibo Hut to Uhuru Peak, descend to Horombo Hut
Start before midnight for the summit climb to Uhuru Peak. After sunrise at the roof of Africa, descend back to Horombo Hut for a well-earned rest.
Day 5: Horombo Hut to Marangu Gate
Descend through the high moorland and rainforest back to Marangu Gate. Receive your summit certificate and transfer to Moshi or Arusha.
