6 Days Kilimanjaro Trek Marangu

Duration: 6 Days / 5 NightsType: Trekking AdventureDestinations: Marangu Route & Uhuru Peak

Shorter and more affordable than longer treks like Lemosho or Machame.

This 6-day Marangu Route trek for 2026/2027 offers a perfect balance between challenge and comfort, making it one of the best ways to summit Kilimanjaro in a relatively short time. Whether you’re a first-time trekker or an experienced climber, this well-structured, scenic, and comfortable route provides an unforgettable once-in-a-lifetime experience on Africa’s highest peak.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Moshi – Marangu Gate – Mandara Hut
Pick up from your Moshi hotel and transfer to Kilimanjaro National Park. Begin the trail in lush rainforest and reach Mandara Hut in the late afternoon.
Day 2: Mandara Hut – Horombo Hut
Hike through moorland and pass the Maundi Crater, with clear views of Kibo Glacier when the weather is good. Arrive at Horombo Hut for your second night.
Day 3: Horombo Hut – Kibo Hut
Climb nearly 1,000 meters to Kibo Hut, moving into alpine desert. Reach the final hut and rest well before your summit attempt.
Day 4: Kibo Hut – Uhuru Peak – Horombo Hut
Start early with a light snack and hot tea, then climb through darkness toward Stella Point and Uhuru Peak. After the summit, descend back to Horombo Hut for a recovery night.
Day 5: Horombo Hut – Marangu Gate – Moshi
Descend back through the high moorland and rainforest to Marangu Gate. Celebrate your achievement, collect your summit certificate, and transfer to Moshi.
Day 6: Departure or Rest in Moshi
Enjoy a relaxed morning in Moshi with time to recover and reflect on your summit. Transfer to the airport or continue to Zanzibar, safari, or onward travel.
