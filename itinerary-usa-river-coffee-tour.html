Usa River Coffee Tour

Duration: 2-4 Hours Type: Guided coffee farm tour Location: Usa River, Arusha

Discover the journey from bean to cup on a guided Arabica coffee tour in Usa River. Visit a local farm, learn traditional growing and roasting methods, and taste fresh coffee with calming village views.

Tour Description

Farm Visit
Begin at a small Arabica coffee farm near Lake Duluti, where your guide introduces you to the coffee plants and explains the cultivation and harvesting methods used locally.
Roasting Demonstration
See traditional roasting over firewood and learn how the beans are transformed into aromatic coffee through small-scale, heritage methods.
Coffee Tasting
Taste fresh brewed Arabica coffee on a terrace overlooking the village and surrounding greenery, enjoying the relaxed atmosphere and crisp air.
Local Culture
Take a short walk through the neighborhood to see daily life in Usa River, meet locals, and feel the community spirit that makes this experience memorable.
Notes
Transport to the activity location is not included. Private taxi transfers from Arusha can be arranged for €60 return, or you may travel independently by public transport. Minimum age is 2 years.

What's Included

  • Local English-speaking guide
  • Coffee farm tour
  • Coffee tasting

What's Excluded

  • Return transport to your accommodation
  • Tips for the guide
  • Lunch
