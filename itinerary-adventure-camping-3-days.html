3 Days Adventure Camping Safari

Duration: 3 Days / 2 NightsType: Budget CampingDestinations: Tarangire, Ngorongoro & Lake Manyara

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Arusha to Tarangire
After breakfast we depart for Tarangire National Park for a game drive, setting camp in the evening beside the tree-lined plains.
Day 2: Ngorongoro Crater
A short transfer leads to the Ngorongoro Conservation Area, with a crater descent and excellent wildlife sightings before heading to camp.
Day 3: Lake Manyara & Return
We spend the morning in Lake Manyara for birdlife and tree-climbing lions, then return to Arusha in the afternoon.
