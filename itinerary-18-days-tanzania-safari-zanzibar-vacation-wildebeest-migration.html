18 Days Tanzania Safari & Zanzibar Vacation

Duration: 18 Days / 17 Nights Type: Private Safari & Beach Journey Destinations: Arusha, Tarangire, Serengeti, Ngorongoro & Zanzibar

This 18-day private safari and beach trip is built for travelers who want to combine Big Five encounters, the Great Wildebeest Migration, and an unhurried Zanzibar finale. From Arusha and Tarangire to the Serengeti and the Ngorongoro Crater, the journey balances wildlife adventure with rest and coastal relaxation.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Arrive in Arusha
Welcome to Tanzania with a smooth airport pickup and a relaxed evening in Arusha.
Day 2: Relaxing Day in Arusha
Enjoy a slow day to settle in, stay at the lodge, or explore local activities around the city.
Day 3: Arusha National Park
Begin your safari with a first game drive in a park known for giraffes, colobus monkeys, and a scenic mix of forest, lake, and crater landscapes.
Day 4: Leisure Day in Arusha
Choose a calm day at the lodge, a local activity, or a gentle outing before the main safari circuit begins.
Day 5: Tarangire National Park
Head to Tarangire to see elephant herds, giant baobabs, and the park’s dramatic river scenery.
Days 6-8: Serengeti National Park
Spend several days exploring the Serengeti, where the plains open up to endless wildlife viewing and the possibility of tracing the Great Migration.
Day 9: Ngorongoro Conservation Area
Visit the Ngorongoro Crater for one of the most extraordinary wildlife days in East Africa.
Day 10: Flight to Zanzibar
Transfer to Zanzibar and begin your beach stay on the island’s white-sand coastline.
Days 11-16: Zanzibar Beach and Culture
Enjoy swimming, snorkeling, dhow cruises, Stone Town, spice plantations, and optional island excursions at your own pace.
Days 17-18: Departure
Use your final days to relax on the beach before your departure from Zanzibar.

What’s Included

  • Airport pickups and transfers
  • Private 4x4 safari vehicle and guide
  • Accommodation on safari and in Zanzibar
  • Park fees and full board during safari
  • Domestic flight to Zanzibar

What’s Excluded

  • International flights
  • Visa fees and travel insurance
  • Personal expenses and tips
  • Optional activities not listed
