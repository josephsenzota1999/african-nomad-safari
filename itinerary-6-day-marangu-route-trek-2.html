6 Days Marangu Route Trek – Kilimanjaro’s Iconic Climb

Duration: 6 Days / 5 NightsType: Trekking AdventureDestinations: Marangu Route & Uhuru Peak

6-Day Marangu Route Trek – Kilimanjaro’s Iconic Climb is one of the most popular trekking routes up to the summit of Mount Kilimanjaro, located in Tanzania.

6-Day Marangu Route Trek – Kilimanjaro’s Iconic Climb provides a slower pace, allowing for better acclimatization and increasing the chances of reaching the summit.

Marangu Route is the most popular, least difficult and the shortest way to Uhuru Peak.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Moshi – Marangu Gate – Mandara Hut
Pick up from your Moshi accommodations and drive to Kilimanjaro National Park. Liaise with your guides and begin the ascent through the rainforest, hiking 5-6 hours while spotting black and white colobus monkeys along the way.
Day 2: Mandara Hut – Horombo Hut
Set off early for a long day of hiking into the alpine zone. You will cover over 11 kilometres to Maundi Crater and, if the weather is clear, enjoy views of Kibo Glacier before settling into Horombo Hut.
Day 3: Horombo Hut – Kibo Hut
Another big day takes you from Horombo Hut to Kibo Hut, climbing nearly 1,000 metres and passing the last watering point at 4,130m before reaching the final hut in the afternoon.
Day 4: Kibo Hut – Uhuru Peak – Horombo Hut
Start just after midnight with tea and a light snack before hiking in darkness toward Stella Point. After dawn, push on for the final ascent to Uhuru Peak, then descend back to Horombo Hut for rest.
Day 5: Horombo Hut – Marangu Gate – Moshi
Enjoy a last breakfast on the mountain and descend nearly 20 kilometres downhill to Marangu Gate. Celebrate with your crew, tip your guides, collect your summit certificate, and return to Moshi or Arusha.
Day 6: Rest Day in Moshi or Departure
Relax in Moshi after your summit, recover with a pleasant morning, and prepare for your onward travel or airport transfer.
