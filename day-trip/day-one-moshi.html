Lake Duluti Adventure: Mountain Biking & Canoeing Day Trip

Duration: 1 Day / 1 Nights Type: Private Tour (Customizable) Destinations: Arusha

Overview

After a quick bike fitting, you will leave the main roads to navigate red-dirt paths through local Meru villages, markets, and lush coffee and banana plantations. As you reach Lake Duluti, you will cycle along the crater rim forest, spotting active birdlife and enjoying views of Mount Meru before winding down with a relaxing lakeside break.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 0: Arrival in Arusha
Welcome to Africa! Upon arrival at Kilimanjaro Airport (JRO), you will be met by our private driver who will transfer you to the Gran Melia Hotel in Arusha. Receive a briefing on your upcoming safari journey.
Day 1:Bike Tour Day Trip in Moshi
The mountain biking tour begins at Lake Duluti, set at the foot of Mt. Meru. After a short briefing, you’ll set off with your guide on a route that takes you along rural paths, lush coffee, markets, banana plantations and through lively villages. Along the way, you’ll experience daily life in the countryside, with children waving as you ride by and farmers tending their crops. The views of Mt. Meru provide a spectacular backdrop, while the quieter dirt roads and trails make for relaxed and enjoyable biking.
