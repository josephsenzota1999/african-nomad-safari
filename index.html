Craft Your Dream African Safari

100% Tailor-made private safaris at guaranteed best prices

An African Safari is the ultimate once-in-a-lifetime adventure! From the vast open plains of the Serengeti and Maasai Mara to the breathtaking wilderness of Ngorongoro Crater, Africa holds wonders unlike anywhere else. After the excitement of tracking the Big Five, relax on the powdery white sand beaches of the coast or test your limits on a highland adventure.

We are the African Nomad specialists for a reason: we operate our own fleet of custom 4x4 safari vehicles and employ experienced local guides who know every track, watering hole, and migration pattern. We guarantee the best price and quality for your private journey.

Browse our popular itineraries below, or get in touch with our travel designers. Let us know which parks and beaches you dream of visiting, and we will craft the perfect proposal for you.
