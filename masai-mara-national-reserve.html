Classic acacia-dotted plains, extraordinary big-cat encounters, and the dramatic northern stage of the Great Migration.

Kenya's legendary wildlife reserve

The Masai Mara National Reserve is one of Africa's most celebrated safari destinations. Its open grasslands, rolling hills, and winding Mara River support an exceptional concentration of wildlife, from large lion prides and stalking cheetahs to elephants, buffalo, giraffes, and hippos.

The Mara forms the northern continuation of the Serengeti ecosystem. During the Great Migration, huge herds move into the reserve in search of fresh grazing, creating unforgettable scenes around the Mara River and its surrounding plains.

Masai Mara highlights

  • Large resident populations of lions and cheetahs
  • Wildebeest and zebra migration crossings
  • Elephants and giraffes across open savannah
  • Sunrise and sunset game drives
  • Authentic Maasai cultural experiences
  • Easy pairing with the Serengeti and Zanzibar

Best time to visit

July to October is popular for dry weather and the arrival of migrating herds, although river crossings are natural events and cannot be guaranteed on a particular day. January to March is often quieter and excellent for big-cat viewing. The Mara offers resident wildlife throughout the year, making it a flexible choice for a Kenya safari.
